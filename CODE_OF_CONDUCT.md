# Unio Collector code of conduct

Unio Collector is a company-maintained, source-available project under the
custom [LICENSE](LICENSE). This code applies to issues, pull requests and other
project-managed community spaces.

Treat people respectfully and professionally. Discuss ideas and code in good
faith, give constructive feedback and respect differing backgrounds and views.

Harassment, abuse, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption and publication
of another person's private information are prohibited. Do not post credentials,
customer evidence or other secrets.

Maintainers may moderate or remove inappropriate content and restrict or remove
participation. For a concern requiring private escalation, use Tevari Cloud's
normal contact mechanism through the [Tevari Cloud contact page](https://tevaricloud.com/contact).
Share only the minimum non-sensitive context needed to explain the concern.
Security vulnerabilities must follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy makes no legal, service-level or response-time commitments.
